## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a modern institutional and analytical GovTech aesthetic, specifically tailored for higher education governance and public administration modernization. It projects academic prestige, technical rigor, and digital transformation transparency.

Targeted at university leadership, faculty deans, administrative staff, and the university community, the visual tone establishes uncompromising authority, clarity, and systematic progress. 

The aesthetic is **Corporate / Modern** elevated by **Subtle Tonal Micro-layers**:
- Pristine white and cool slate surfaces that deliver high data density without visual fatigue.
- High-contrast authoritative headings anchored by deep navy and slate tones.
- Institutional crimson accents that evoke traditional academic identity, paired with precise status indicators for operational transparency.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around the institutional crimson heritage of the university, balanced by deep, authoritative corporate dark blues for structural UI elements.

- **Primary (`#8B1419`):** Institutional crimson. Applied to brand identity touchpoints, active states, key interactive links, and principal accent markers.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A`) & Tertiary (`#1E293B`):** Deep Navy and Slate. Used for primary typography, headers, shell navigation bars, and data visualization baselines.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`):** Cool Slate. Serves as secondary copy, inactive icons, axis labels, and metric denominators.
- **Surfaces & Borders:** Clean canvas background (`#F8FAFC`) with elevated card containers (`#FFFFFF`) framed by precise slate borders (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Semantic Indicators:** Functional, high-visibility semantic tokens:
  - `status-success` (`#10B981`) for completed milestones and positive KPI compliance.
  - `status-warning` (`#F59E0B`) for ongoing processes and pending verifications.
  - `status-info` (`#2563EB`) for digital workflows and active cloud services.
  - `status-purple` (`#7C3AED`) for strategic research and innovative initiatives.

## Typography

The design system utilizes **Inter** across all typographical scales to ensure clarity across dense data dashboards and tabular reports.

- **Numerals & Metrics:** Large metrics (`title-kpi`) apply tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent jitter across dynamic updates.
- **Section & Category Eyebrows:** Styled with uppercase `label-md` or `label-sm` with explicit letter tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to frame module context without competing with main metrics.
- **Headlines:** Display and title roles feature tight negative tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.03em`) for a structured, publication-grade tone.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid designed for analytical portals, capped at a maximum width of `1440px` for optimal viewing ergonomics on high-resolution displays.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (≥1200px):** 12 columns, `gutter-lg` (32px), `margin` (32px). KPI grids form 3 or 6 equal units.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, `gutter` (24px), `margin` (24px). KPI modules transition to a 2x3 layout.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, `gutter-sm` (16px), `margin-mobile` (16px). Cards stack sequentially to preserve touch target integrity.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Content zones are separated by consistent `space-2xl` intervals. Internal card content maintains a compact 16px to 24px (`space-md` to `space-lg`) padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through multi-layer, low-opacity ambient shadows paired with explicit 1px outline borders, avoiding heavy skeuomorphism in favor of crisp GovTech clarity.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background color `#F8FAFC`, zero shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Shells):** `#FFFFFF` fill with a `1px solid #E2E8F0` border and subtle ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Containers):** `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`, with border color transitioning to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Dropdowns, Popovers & Menus):** `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`, bordered by `#E2E8F0`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a balanced **Rounded (`2`)** geometric standard. Cards, navigation bars, and inputs use `0.5rem` (8px) radius to combine modern software aesthetics with institutional structure. Badges and status pills leverage full pill rounding (`9999px`) to visually separate qualitative metadata from structural cards.

## Components

### Header & Identity Bar
- Clean, fixed top navigation featuring official university insignia on the left, paired with an institutional divider line and the Modernization Unit descriptor.
- Nav items display active states through a bold crimson bottom indicator line (`3px`) with text tinted to deep slate (`#0F172A`).

### KPI Progress Cards
- **Architecture:** Crisp white containers divided into an upper header (icon container + uppercase category badge + link arrow), a central progress zone, and a bottom action bar.
- **Radial Rings:** Radial meters use clean SVG arcs with a base track in light slate (`#F1F5F9`) and the active arc rendered in the designated category color (e.g., Crimson for Normativa, Blue for Procesos, Green for Simplificación). The interior displays the completion percentage in bold `headline-md`.
- **Metrics Pair:** Beside the meter, large integer figures display the primary count (bold, `#0F172A`) over total baseline metrics (`body-sm`, neutral slate).

### Status Badges & Pills
- Rendered in pill form (`rounded-full`) with a soft tinted background (10% tint of the status color) and high-contrast bold text.
- Must include a leading 6px circular dot indicator matching the semantic state (Emerald for "En avance", Amber for "En desarrollo", Gray for "Progresivo").

### Action & Navigation Cards
- Interactive project cards use an outer border `#E2E8F0` that transitions to primary `#8B1419` on hover.
- Right-aligned directional arrows are nested within soft circular containers (`#F1F5F9`) that translate 3px on hover.

### Buttons & Inputs
- **Primary Action:** Background `#8B1419`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded 8px, hover background `#630C10`.
- **Secondary Action:** White background with `#E2E8F0` border, `#1E293B` text, hover background `#F8FAFC`.
- **Inputs:** Height 40px, border `#E2E8F0`, interior padding 12px, focus ring with 2px crimson outline offset by 2px white.
